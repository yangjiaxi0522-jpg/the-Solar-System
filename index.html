理论应用：四个重点Theoretical application: four key principles

重点 1Key idea 1 · Lesson 1 · 五种模态Lesson 1 · five modes

同一件事，交给最擅长的表达方式Let each mode do what it is best at

每种表达方式都有它擅长和做不来的事。一张图能在一秒内说明「谁在谁的里面」「谁比谁大」，但它说不出「88 天」；文字能准确说出「88 天」，却没法表示方向。所以设计的问题不是「怎么把同一句话写两遍」，而是「这件事谁来说最合适」。 Every way of expressing something has strengths and limits. A picture shows "who is inside whom" or "who is bigger" in a second but it cannot say "88 days". Writing says "88 days" exactly but cannot show a direction. So the design question is not "how do I say this twice?", it is "which mode says this best?"

首页就是这样分工的：位置和画面负责轨道顺序与大小比例；文字只写名字和数字；声音用一句 15 秒的口语解释关键点；动作交给手指——点行星、拖行星；动画只做一件事，就是公转本身，因为那是静图最说不清的。 The home screen splits the work: position and picture carry the order of the orbits and the size ratio; writing carries only names and numbers; sound gives the key idea in one 15-second spoken sentence; gesture belongs to the hand — tap a planet, drag a planet; and animation does exactly one job, the orbit itself, because that is the thing a still picture cannot explain.

八颗行星的公转方向实测全部一致（逆时针），大小顺序也是真实的（水星最小、木星最大）。同一个大小模型还被复用到三处——首页动画、拖拽游戏的虚线圆圈、复习页那排天体——所以"木星最大"这件事不会换个页面就变一个说法。 All eight planets were measured moving the same way (anticlockwise), and their sizes keep the real order — Mercury smallest, Jupiter largest. The same size model is reused in three places — the home animation, the dashed circles in the drag game, and the row of planets on the summary page — so "Jupiter is the biggest" never changes its story from one screen to the next.

重点 2Key idea 2 · Lesson 2 · 视觉语法Lesson 2 · visual grammar

版面的位置本身也在说话The layout is talking too

同一个页面上，上方通常读作「理想、想象」，下方读作「真实、事实」；左边读作「我已经知道的」，右边读作「新来的」。看的人不用别人提醒，就会按这个顺序读。 On a page, the top half usually reads as ideal or imagined, and the bottom half as real and factual; the left side reads as what I already know, and the right side as what is new. Viewers follow that order without being told.

每个学习模块都用同一套版式：上方一条金色「想象一下」；下面三栏，左边「你已经知道的」，中间是这一段的插图，右边「今天的新发现」。谁被学就用最大的插图（显著性），每一块用各自的边框隔开（框定），向导正面对着你看、用聊天的语气说话。 Every lesson module repeats one layout: a gold "Imagine…" strip on top; then three columns — "what you already know" on the left, this module's artwork in the middle, "new today" on the right. The planet being learned gets the biggest picture (salience), each band is separated by its own border (framing), and the guide faces you and talks like a person.

1440×900 窗口下实测（视口宽 1414）：左栏 x=74、插图栏 x=458、右栏 x=968，已知在左、新知在右；金色的「想象一下」横条在 y=230，整条压在左右两栏（y=291）之上——位置关系和理论要求的一致。原来模块 1 底部还有一条「真实数字」的 Real 数据带（用来对照 Ideal–Real 上下轴），为了让页面对 8–10 岁的学生更好读，已经整块删掉，所以这一条现在讲的是 Given–New 横轴 + Ideal 上沿。 Measured in a 1440 × 900 window (1414 px viewport): the known column sits at x = 74, the artwork column at x = 458 and the new column at x = 968 — known on the left, new on the right; the gold "Imagine…" strip sits at y = 230, above both columns (y = 291). The positions match the theory. Module 1 used to carry an extra "Real numbers" strip along the bottom (that was the Real half of the Ideal–Real axis); it was removed so the page reads more easily for 8–10 year olds, so this card now documents the Given–New horizontal axis plus the Ideal strip on top.

重点 3Key idea 3 · Lesson 3 · 减少外部负荷Lesson 3 · cutting extraneous load

别让无关的东西占脑子Do not let decoration use up the brain

工作记忆很小。和内容无关的花样、以及「屏幕上一大段、喇叭里念同一段」，都会挤掉本该用来理解的空间，所以这些要做减法。 Working memory is small. Decoration that has nothing to do with the content, and hearing the very paragraph you are reading, both use up the space that understanding needs. So these get removed.

所以：没有背景音乐，也没有装饰性动画；只有重要词才用金色高亮；旁白说的是「短版口语」，不照读屏幕（字幕默认关掉，需要时才开）；每张说明都贴在它描述的东西旁边——行星的名字就在行星图卡片里，点行星弹出的简介就出现在那颗行星旁边。 So: no background music and no decorative animation; only the words that matter get a gold highlight; the voice says a short spoken version instead of reading the screen out loud (captions stay off unless you ask); and every label sits next to the thing it describes — a planet's name lives inside its own card, and the card you open appears beside that planet.

14 组关键文字/底色的对比度实测 5.5–16.5:1（全部达到 WCAG AA）；1440、1100、430px 三种宽度下都没有横向滚动；放错位置只给提示，不会判定失败。行星资料卡也一样：点开木星时卡片落在整行星球上方，和九颗天体的球面相交面积为 0%，没有一个知识点需要靠"挡住别的星球"来换取位置。 All 14 key text-on-background pairs measure 5.5–16.5:1 (WCAG AA); there is no sideways scrolling at 1440, 1100 or 430 px; and a wrong drop gives a hint rather than a failure. The planet cards follow the same rule: opening Jupiter puts the card above the whole row of planets, with 0 % overlap on any of the nine bodies — no fact is ever placed by covering another planet up.

重点 4Key idea 4 · Lesson 3 · 管理本质负荷Lesson 3 · pacing the essential load

一次只给一小片，让他自己按下一步One small piece at a time, in the learner's own order

一次学完太阳系太多了。把内容切成小片、让孩子自己决定什么时候继续，会轻松很多；正式内容之前先把要用的词学会，也能省下不少力气。 Learning the whole Solar System at once is too much. Cutting it into small pieces, and letting the child decide when to go on, makes it much easier; learning the words they will need before the main content saves effort too.

内容分成「预习 → 2 个模块（太阳系简介 / 太阳与八大行星）→ 3 个练习 → 总结」，一次只出现一段；这一段太长时底部会浮出「上一段 / 下一段」，所以想继续不用先滚到底。正式认识行星之前，先用四张卡片教四个词：恒星、行星、公转、卫星。关键解释用十几秒的语音，而不是一大段文字。总结页也按同一原则分成小块：先看图回忆、再点回知识页对答案，最后才是自评清单和迁移小挑战。 The content is cut into pre-training, two modules (an overview, then the Sun and the eight planets), three activities and a summary — one piece on screen at a time, with a floating "previous / next" control whenever a section runs long, so nobody has to scroll to the bottom to continue. Before any planet appears, four cards teach the four words they will need: star, planet, orbit, moon. Key explanations arrive as a short voice clip rather than a paragraph. The summary page follows the same rule: recall from the picture, tap back to the lesson to check, and only then the self-check list and the transfer challenge.

1440×900 下五个学习页面各占 0.7–0.9 屏（首页 0.8 / 预习 0.7 / 分段学习 0.8 / 动手练习 0.8 / 总结 0.9），一屏读完不用来回滚；手机 430px 下最长的页面 1.7 屏。 At 1440×900 each of the five learning screens takes 0.7–0.9 of a screen (home 0.8, pre-training 0.7, modules 0.8, practice 0.8, summary 0.9) — one screen, no scrolling back and forth; on a 430 px phone the longest screen is 1.7 screens.
